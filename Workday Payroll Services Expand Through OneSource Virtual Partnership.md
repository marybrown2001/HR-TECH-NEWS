HR technology giant Workday has expanded its strategic reseller partnership with OneSource Virtual to deliver managed [Workday payroll services](https://hrtech-news.com/workday-payroll-services-expand-through-onesource-virtual/?utm_source=gemini) directly through Workday’s direct sales channel. The new distribution agreement allows mid-market organizations across the United States to add payroll administration and payroll tax services directly to their platform subscription under a unified purchasing agreement. Designed specifically for new Workday Payroll clients with 3,500 or fewer employees, the offering streamlines procurement while delivering specialized operational expertise within the core Human Capital Management environment.

The expanded relationship addresses a critical operational challenge faced by growing human resources departments. While modern cloud platforms automate core calculations, managing payroll taxes, regulatory filings, and garnishment compliance requires dedicated operational resources. By bundling expert payroll services into the platform contract, mid-sized businesses can scale their operations without building complex internal administrative teams.

**Delivering Native Payroll Processing via In-Tenant Architecture**
-------------------------------------------------------------------

Unlike traditional business process outsourcing models that export data to third-party software, OneSource Virtual operates through patented in-tenant technology. This native architecture allows service teams to perform payroll processing and tax reconciliation directly inside the customer's secure Workday environment. Keeping data within the primary system eliminates external file transfers, reduces security risks, and maintains real-time visibility for internal HR leaders.

Operating as an exclusive ecosystem partner since 2008, OneSource Virtual currently manages payroll operations for more than 1,500 organizations and processes over 6.5 million pay transactions monthly. The company’s specialized teams manage complex wage calculations, off-cycle disbursements, year-end tax reporting, and multi-state compliance. This embedded operational support ensures high payment accuracy while maintaining strict data governance across corporate financial systems.

**Empowering Mid-Market Employers with Enterprise Capabilities**
----------------------------------------------------------------

Mid-sized enterprises often face sophisticated workforce requirements but lack the expansive operational teams of global corporations. The direct-sales arrangement allows these growing companies to access enterprise-grade payroll infrastructure through a simplified vendor relationship. Contracting directly through Workday compresses procurement timelines, eliminates separate integration projects, and accelerates initial deployment schedules.

Darren Koch, General Manager for Workday GO, highlighted that combining Workday's unified HR platform with specialized payments expertise enables mid-market customers to reduce administrative overhead and focus on strategic business initiatives. As competition for talent increases, reliable payroll execution remains foundational to employee trust and operational stability.

**Executive Alignment and Industry Convergence**
------------------------------------------------

Brian Levey, Chief Sales Officer for OneSource Virtual, emphasized that the expanded agreement reflects a shared commitment to customer success by meeting employers in the procurement channels they already use. The partnership highlights a broader trend across enterprise software, where major platforms integrate specialized managed services to deliver end-to-end operational outcomes.

By unifying cloud software and managed administrative services, Workday and OneSource Virtual create a scalable foundation for modern workforce management. This ecosystem approach ensures mid-market organizations can navigate evolving payroll tax regulations, maintain operational agility, and support hybrid workforces without increasing internal administrative costs.
